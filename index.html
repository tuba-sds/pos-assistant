POS Assistant

A single-user personal assistant. Not a public service.

POS Assistant is a private tool that reads one person's own Gmail and Google Calendar, sorts what arrived, and prepares a morning summary plus draft replies for that person to review. It is used by its author only.

What it does

  • Reads the owner's own mail and calendar to work out what needs attention.
  • Writes a daily summary to the owner's private notes and private Discord server.
  • Prepares drafts in the owner's own Gmail account.

What it never does

  • It does not send, reply to, forward or delete any email.
  • It does not create, edit or delete calendar events.
  • It does not contact anyone other than the owner.
  • It has no other users, and accepts no sign-ups.

Sending is impossible by construction: the application requests no send scope, and a local guard blocks any send or delete call.
